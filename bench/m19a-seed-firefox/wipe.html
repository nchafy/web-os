wipe the OPFS volume

The negative control for bench/m19a-seed-firefox/firefox-session.py. It takes the writable volume away so the next boot of the desktop meets a fresh one and seeds a new copy of the base over what the reader had — which is the defect docs/CONSTRAINTS.md 28 forbids, and the world the persists clause has to be able to see.

Nothing happens on load. The driver calls wipeOpfs().
